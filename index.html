The Role of English Listening on English Speaking Fluency

A research project exploring the role of English listening in developing speaking fluency among EFL learners.

Sayed Haroon Ahmadi · Sayed Ozair Sadat · Sayed Mujtaba Hamdard
Suhail Frotan · Ali Behroz Bassieg · Abozar Sarwari
About Objectives Methodology Findings Conclusion References

About the Research

This study investigated the role of English listening in English speaking fluency. The research focused on listening to English materials such as podcasts, interviews, conversations, songs, movies and other audio or video resources.

The study explored how frequent exposure to spoken English may support speaking fluency, vocabulary, pronunciation and confidence among English language learners.

150 Participants
20 Questionnaire Items
2025 Research Year

Research Objectives

  • To explore the role of English listening on speaking fluency.
  • To identify listening skills.

Research Question

What is the role of English listening in speaking fluency?

Methodology

The study used a quantitative research method. Data were collected through a questionnaire designed to examine learners' perceptions and experiences regarding English listening and speaking fluency.

Participants: 150 participants took part in the study, including male and female learners.
Age range: Participants represented different age groups, from 10 to over 30 years old.
Languages: Participants included Dari, Pashto and Uzbek speakers.
Data Analysis: Microsoft Excel and SPSS were used for data analysis.

Key Findings

Listening and fluency: Most participants agreed or strongly agreed that listening to English contributes to better speaking fluency.
Podcasts: Participants reported that listening to podcasts helps them speak fluently on different topics.
Different accents: Many participants agreed that exposure to different English accents helps them speak better in various situations.
English songs: Participants generally viewed English songs as useful for developing and improving speaking skills.
Real conversations: Listening to real English conversations was associated with helping learners think and respond.
Daily listening: Many participants agreed that daily English listening makes their speaking more natural.
Listening and speaking: The study found that participants generally perceived listening and speaking as closely related skills.

Selected Results

The questionnaire results showed strong positive responses to several statements related to English listening and speaking.

88% More listening → better speaking
88.7% Practice → confidence
87.3% Slang & idioms → casual speaking
86% Enjoyable listening → fluency

Conclusion

The findings of the study underscore the importance of listening as an effective strategy for improving English speaking fluency among Afghan EFL learners.

Regular exposure to English through podcasts, movies, conversations, interviews and other listening materials can support learners in developing speaking fluency in a natural and enjoyable way.

The research also highlights the importance of daily practice and access to appropriate English learning resources.

Research Limitations

  • Limited number of female participants.
  • Limited research time.
  • Limited research experience among researchers.
  • Limited resources for identifying participants.
  • Financial and time constraints.
